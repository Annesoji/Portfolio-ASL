DICP - UX UI Designer / PO

• User Journey: Currently, users must navigate between three different agencies (CCISM, DICP, and the Treasury) to create, modify, or pay for their business tax (Patente).
• Manual & Paper-Based Processes: Key administrative actions, such as business creation or cessation, remain paper-based.
• Data Inconsistency: Information required for business updates is scattered across multiple websites, leading to outdated or inconsistent data.
• Digital Gap• : Agencies lack modern digital tools, which severely limits the digitization of administrative procedures.
• Operational Inefficiency: Unclear roles between administrations and a high volume of manual data entry increase the risk of errors and reduce overall efficiency.

Prototype

DICP - UX UI Designer / PO

User Journey by user type
Optimization opportunities:
• Developing a centralized platform that consolidates business creation, tax declaration, calculation, payment, and history within a single interface.
• Improving the overall user experience through dedicated personal spaces, real-time notifications, and comprehensive history logs.
• Ensuring automated and consistent data synchronization between the CCISM, DICP, and the Treasury.
• Reducing paper-based workflows to minimize manual data entry errors and increase operational efficiency
• Creating a centralized dashboard for administrative agents to track payments and case progress in real-time.

DICP - UX UI Designer / PO

As part of a broader IT modernization initiative, the redesign of the business tax contribution process (Patente) centers on the creation of a unified digital portal. The primary objective is to streamline the contributor journey, with a specific focus on business creation, while providing users with a clearer understanding of tax calculations and all procedures related to managing their professional activity.

DICP - UX UI Designer / PO

Key features of the Future Portal:
• Consolidating features and data from DICP, CCISM, and the Treasury into a single access point.
• Tailored interfaces for Business Owner, Artisans, and Freelancers.
• Providing live guidance and support for users.
• Enabling seamless online payments and real-time transaction tracking.
• Providing full visibility into past administrative actions.
• Allowing users to independently create or modify their business information.
• Clarifying tax calculation logic and providing a built-in simulation tool for business taxes (Patente).
• Automated alerts for upcoming deadlines, required payments, or overdue status.
